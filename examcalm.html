Stay Calm & Confident

Simple ways to stay focused and confident before and during examinations.

📌 Before the Exam

  • Prepare your materials in advance.
  • Get enough sleep.
  • Avoid last-minute panic.
  • Revise only important points.

🧘 Stay Focused

1 Take a few deep breaths.
2 Read every question carefully.
3 Start with questions you know.
4 Manage your time properly.
5 Review your answers before submitting.

💡 Remember

Stay positive and trust your preparation. If a question looks difficult, do not panic. Move to the next question and return to it later.
