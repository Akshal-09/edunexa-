Complete Syllabus

A simple strategy to complete your syllabus before the examination.

📌 Why Complete the Syllabus?

Completing the syllabus early gives you enough time for revision and practice.

It also helps you identify difficult topics and spend more time improving them.

⚙️ Step-by-Step Plan

1 List all subjects and units.
2 Divide topics into easy and difficult.
3 Start with important and difficult topics.
4 Set daily completion targets.
5 Keep separate time for revision.

💡 Useful Tips

  • Follow a realistic timetable.
  • Do not postpone difficult topics.
  • Mark completed topics.
  • Revise each unit after completing it.
