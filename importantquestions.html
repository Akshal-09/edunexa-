Important Questions

Focus on important concepts and frequently asked questions.

📌 How to Identify Important Questions?

Review previous year papers, class notes and questions discussed by your faculty.

Focus on repeated questions and concepts that carry higher marks.

⚙️ Preparation Steps

1 Collect important questions.
2 Group questions by topic.
3 Understand the concept behind each question.
4 Practice writing answers.
5 Revise them before the exam.

💡 Important Tip

Do not depend only on predicted questions. Use important questions as a revision tool and make sure you understand the complete syllabus.
