Time Management

Manage your study time and examination time more effectively.

📌 Why Time Management?

Good time management helps you complete your syllabus without unnecessary stress.

It also allows enough time for revision, practice and relaxation.

⚙️ Simple Method

1 Prepare a daily study timetable.
2 Give more time to difficult subjects.
3 Study in focused sessions.
4 Take short breaks between sessions.
5 Review your progress every day.

💡 Tips

  • Avoid unnecessary mobile usage.
  • Do one task at a time.
  • Keep daily goals realistic.
  • Reserve time for revision.
