Exam Revision

Use a simple revision strategy to remember important concepts effectively.

📌 Why Revision is Important?

Revision strengthens your memory and helps you recall concepts during examinations.

⚙️ Revision Method

1 Read the topic carefully.
2 Create short notes.
3 Recall the concept without looking.
4 Solve related questions.
5 Repeat difficult topics.

🌟 Revision Tips

  • Start revision early.
  • Use short notes and diagrams.
  • Practice important questions.
  • Do not study everything at the last minute.
