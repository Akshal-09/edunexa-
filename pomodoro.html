Pomodoro Technique

A simple method to improve focus and manage study time effectively.

📌 What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management method that divides study time into short, focused sessions.

It helps students stay focused and avoid distractions while studying for long hours.

⚙️ How It Works

1 Choose a topic to study.
2 Study with full concentration for 25 minutes.
3 Take a 5-minute break.
4 Repeat the process four times.
5 Take a longer break of 15–30 minutes.

🌟 Benefits

  • Improves concentration
  • Reduces study stress
  • Helps manage time
  • Reduces distractions
  • Improves study productivity

💡 Useful Tips

  • Keep your phone away while studying.
  • Choose one topic for each session.
  • Use breaks to relax your mind.
  • Track the topics you complete.
